# Portfolio copy

All descriptions below refer to current Phase 2A / 3D development. The latest stable binary is v0.2.0, which has the earlier Observatory, History & Causality feature set.

## Card short version

**Project CIVITAS**  
A deterministic living-city simulation.

Hundreds of autonomous citizens study, work, form households, change careers and move through social classes over decades. Major statistics can be investigated through actual citizens, companies, households and recorded events; explicit causal links are distinguished from associations.

## Hero version

**Project CIVITAS**  
A city simulation where every number has a life behind it.

CIVITAS simulates hundreds of deterministic citizens across decades of education, employment, housing, household formation and social mobility, then lets you investigate the recorded evidence behind how the city changed.

## Technology

TypeScript · React · Electron · Three.js · Agent-Based Simulation · Deterministic Replay

## Current Phase 2A development validation

- 100–1000 initial simulated citizens.
- 110 automated tests.
- 100-seed five-year stability validation.
- 250 / 1000 initial citizens × 30-year persistence / replay validation.
- 1000 initial citizens × 100-year demographic stress test.

These figures do not describe the stable v0.2.0 release acceptance. Long-run demographics are not calibrated to a real city. Native desktop shutdown acceptance remains blocked.
